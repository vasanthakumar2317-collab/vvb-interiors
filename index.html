About VVB

One founder, one site visit, one point of contact.

"Every space we build is measured on-site, drawn to scale, and finished to a standard we'd accept in our own home."

— Vasanthakumar Perumal, Founder

VVB Interior Designing & Aluminium Fabrication is run by Vasanthakumar Perumal from Vellalapatti, Dharmapuri District. With 14 years of overall experience in the trade and 8 years running VVB independently, the practice covers both residential and commercial interior fit-outs alongside aluminium fabrication work.

From modular kitchens and wardrobes to office interiors and pooja rooms, every project is handled directly — from measurement and material selection through to final installation — so clients deal with one person who knows the job end to end.
